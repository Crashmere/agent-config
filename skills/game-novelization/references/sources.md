# Sources and adaptation decisions

This skill reorganizes methods around the user's requirements to faithfully recreate multiple games' main stories, match original style, use walkthroughs and videos, and allocate detail appropriately. The unified workflow was originally expressed in independently written Chinese guidance and does not require installing or invoking upstream skills. The author repositories below were checked on 2026-10-06; their code and runtime environments are not dependencies.

Added domain constraints include edition and route isolation, main-story event-to-chapter coverage tables, separate character-knowledge and reader-revelation boundaries, literary conversion of game mechanics, original-style profiles, video scene evidence, and decisions on narrative emphasis. Methods for reducing formulaic prose are consolidated in `prose-revision.md` for writing and review to share. Protection of certainty applies to character misbeliefs, guesses, promises, and observation timing so polishing does not change the plot. Examples are teaching passages authored for this skill.

Scene and transition development, expansion at the original action's location, and post-expansion checks of space, held objects, knowledge, and paragraph references come from recurring problems in actual long-form revision. Later rounds added choosing revision levels from the overall reading experience, preserving the circumstances of people behind records, checking recollections and callbacks, and comparing the actual effects of old and new prose. Subsequent revision added new-reader comprehension gaps—names arriving after things, motivation existing only in the player's mind, callbacks without setup, retrospective narration leaking later knowledge, names drifting across chapters, and outside-game vocabulary—as well as causes of walkthrough-like narration, whole-book sentence-pattern habits, baseline comparisons for further revision, external-document write-back, and handoff procedures. These experiences inform judgment rather than define an exhaustive writing checklist. They do not bind the skill to one work's viewpoint, chapter count, length, imagery, or game mechanics, and volume of changes is not a quality measure.

At the user's request, final works deliver only novel prose. Upstream permanent research libraries and diagnostic reports become separate temporary records used as needed for the current task and are not delivered with the work. Maintain according to these responsibilities and the user's current requirements; do not reintroduce conflicting formulas or dependencies merely because an upstream source changes.
